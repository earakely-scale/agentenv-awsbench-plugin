# POST-2024-031 streamroute shard-map builds failing (heap)

_Incident OPS-3172, 2024-08-13. Author: Elena Sokolova. Status: closed._

**Impact.** For about 50 minutes, shard maps on 9 of 72 hosts went STALE and then BROKEN; ledger lag on two cells reached
14 minutes. No data lost.

**Cause.** The shard-map builder loads the full lease table into the heap. After the 72-host expansion, builds needed
more than the 4 GB heap: `java.lang.OutOfMemoryError: Java heap space`, with full GCs back to back at 3.9 of 4 GB in the
GC log during every build.

**Fix.** `/streamroute/prod/jvm/xmx` 4g -> 6g (PLAT-1874), rolling restart one AZ at a time. Builds recovered on restart.

**Action items.** RB-SR-04 (done). Heap-after-GC panel on the streamroute dashboard (done). mem_used_percent alarm on
vl-streamroute-asg (done).
