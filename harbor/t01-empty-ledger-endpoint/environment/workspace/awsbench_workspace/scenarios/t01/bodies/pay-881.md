**checkout-api: ledger-client circuit breaker, Retry-After and a durable posting outbox**

- Breaker opens at 50 % failures over 20 calls, open 10 s, half-open probes.
- A posting that cannot reach ledger is written to the outbox (DynamoDB `vl-ledger-outbox`) and replayed by the replayer at
  `/vl/prod/checkout-api/outbox/replay_rate` req/s fleet-wide once ledger answers.
- Metric `Vantleaf/Checkout OutboxPending`.

**Release note (RELEASE.md, PR #1840):** Revisions before :412 do not read the outbox; rolling back strands pending entries
(authorized, unposted payments) until roll-forward. Drain `OutboxPending` to 0 before any rollback.
