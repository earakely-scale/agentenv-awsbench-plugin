### What T01 asks of an on-call agent

At 02:31 a delayed endpointd enactor writes stale plan 48177 over a newer one, and ten seconds later another enactor's
cleanup deletes it, with every record of `ledger.internal`. With the ledger's name empty, checkout success falls to
4 %. The page reaches the agent at 02:40, and from then on it is the incident commander until the window closes at
05:10, when the end state is graded.

Every point a run lost is labelled.
