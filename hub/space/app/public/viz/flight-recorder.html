About this data (T03). Every lane comes from a recorded run of the AwsBench T03 environment: the agent lane from the aws server's meter and action log (each call's virtual cost of 20, 45 or 75 s) with arguments and results from the agent's session; routing share, per-cell ledger lag and backlog, the ASG, concurrent restarts, cellmap load-shed, the canary and settlement from the world's exported series (one sample a minute, ten seconds for routing share and restarts, shown every 30 s); host states by state and the sample host's threads against its limit_nproc from an in-process replay of the run's own calls through the world package, which matches the run's exported host counts at every minute; the hold's armed state from the same replay; people, pages and world events from the timeline and the workspace's records; checks from the grader's rows. The stretch before 08:15 is the scenario's own presimulation from 06:00, the same in every run. Which checks a call "bears on" is a heuristic over its tool and arguments. The reference is the scripted oracle path replayed in process; its outcome rows are the grader's own rules on the replayed world. The T03 runs are development runs on a build whose env bugs are still being fixed.

About this data (T01). Every lane comes from a recorded run of the AwsBench T01 environment, not a simulation of one: the agent lane from the aws server's meter and action log (each call's virtual cost of 20, 45 or 75 s) with arguments and results from the agent's session; checkout, ledger-writer load and capacity, backlog, host recovery and the DNS record from the world's exported series (one sample a minute, ten seconds for the DNS state, shown every 30 s); people, pages and world events from the timeline and the workspace's records; checks from the grader's rows. The 02:30–02:40 stretch is the scenario's seeded prologue, back-filled. Which checks a call "bears on" is a heuristic over its tool and arguments, not the grader's attribution. The reference is the scripted oracle path replayed through the world package in process. Runs on older builds are development runs: their env bugs are fixed since, and the final runs will replace them.
