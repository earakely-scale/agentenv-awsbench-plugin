About this data. Every cell is a streamroute host of a recorded AwsBench T03 run. The world export keeps per-minute host counts, not each host's state, so each run's own calls (the aws server's action log at the times the meter charged them) are replayed through the world package in process, with in-memory AWS and workspace fakes; the replay's per-minute counts match the run's export at every minute, and the board shows the replay's per-host states every 30 s. The sample host's thread count against its limit comes from the same replay. Lag, backlog, the ASG, the canary and settlement come from the world's exported series; colleagues, pages and approvals from the workspace export; checks from the grader's rows. The reference is the scripted oracle path, replayed the same way; its process and communication marks are the ones it is built to pass. The T03 runs shown are development runs on a build whose env bugs are still being fixed.
